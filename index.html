FAQ

Before you reach out.

Is scanning my systems even legal?

Yes — every engagement starts with a signed Rules of Engagement and Authorization Letter covering scope, time window, and data handling. Scans are passive-first and never touch anything outside the agreed scope. No signed authorization, no scan — that's a hard rule, not a formality.

What if the scan finds nothing?

A clean scan is still a report: you get a coverage summary, a confidence statement, and a clear picture of what was and wasn't in scope — never just "we found nothing."

How fast is delivery, really?

Snapshot reports typically land in 3–5 hours of active work. AI/LLM assessments run 2–5 days depending on system complexity. Watch alerts fire as new findings appear.

How is client data handled?

Data minimization by default: short retention, redaction where possible, encryption at rest, and auto-purge after engagement close.

Can international clients work with Cindrasec?

Yes — global engagements are billed in USD via Payoneer or Wise, delivered in English, and treated as an export of service.

How is this different from a generic pentest agency?

Automation keeps marginal cost near zero, so pricing stays a fraction of a manual, one-time pentest — while Watch turns security into an ongoing relationship instead of a single invoice.
